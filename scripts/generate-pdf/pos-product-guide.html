Aaj Cash & Carry POS Section 1: Executive Overview

Executive Summary

Why standard cloud-based POS systems fail in Pakistani retail environments, and how Aaj POS is purpose-built for local shopkeepers.

The Real-World Retail Problem in Pakistan

Most modern POS systems are web-based (cloud apps requiring constant monthly subscriptions in USD). In ordinary Pakistani commercial markets (e.g. Lahore, Karachi, Rawalpindi, Faisalabad), shopkeepers face three critical issues:

  • Frequent Internet & Power Fluctuations: When internet slows down or disconnects, cloud POS systems freeze, halting customer checkout queues and causing severe revenue loss.
  • Data Privacy & Tax Concerns: Shopkeepers do not want their exact daily profit margins, supplier purchase costs, or customer udhaar registers uploaded to external cloud servers.
  • Recurring SaaS Fees: Store owners reject paying monthly or annual subscription fees for software that holds their business data hostage.

The Solution: Aaj Cash & Carry POS

Aaj Cash & Carry POS is an offline-first Windows desktop application. It requires zero internet connectivity to execute sales, update stock, register customers, or print receipts. The store owner owns the software and all database records 100% locally.

Architecture Diagram

Security & Data Flow Isolation

The application strictly follows Chromium context isolation security standards:

  • Renderer (React Frontend): Runs in a sandboxed web view with nodeIntegration: false and contextIsolation: true. It cannot directly touch disk or execute system commands.
  • Preload Bridge (preload.js): Exposes only typed, strictly validated IPC invoke channels under window.api.
  • Main Process (main.js + database.js): Executes in Node.js, directly managing the local SQLite database and thermal receipt hardware.

Database Integrity: SQLite WAL Mode

The database utilizes Write-Ahead Logging (WAL mode):

  • Crash Resistance: If power suddenly cuts off during a sale in a Pakistani shop, existing records are never corrupted. Pending transactions roll back cleanly.
  • Concurrent Reads & Writes: Cashier billing operations can write sales records while Admin analytics simultaneously read reports without locking the database.

Aaj Cash & Carry POS Section 3: POS & Digital Khata

Smart POS Checkout & Digital Khata

Engineered for rapid cashier throughput during peak rush hours, integrated seamlessly with a Pakistani customer udhaar register.

1. Cashier POS Billing Module

Barcode Scanner Integration

Supports all standard USB & wireless 1D/2D barcode guns. Global keystroke interception ensures barcodes are detected and added to cart within 0.1 seconds without requiring the cashier to click the search box first.

Multi-Item Cart & Discounts

Real-time subtotal, percentage discounts, item quantity editing, and automated change return calculator (e.g. Total Rs. 780, Received Rs. 1,000 → Return Rs. 220).

2. Pakistani Digital Khata (Customer Credit Ledger)

In Pakistani neighbourhood retail, over 40% to 60% of total revenue runs on customer trust and monthly credit ("Udhaar"). Aaj POS replaces manual paper notebooks with a tamper-proof digital ledger.

Credit Limit Enforcement

Every customer can be assigned a maximum Udhaar limit (e.g. Rs. 15,000). If a credit sale exceeds this limit, the POS displays a prominent amber warning badge or blocks the sale per shop settings.

Instant Customer Search

Cashiers can search customers by name or mobile number with live balance indicators. If the customer is new, an inline modal allows adding them in 5 seconds without clearing the cart.

3. "Who Owes Me Money?" Outstanding Dues View

The dedicated Outstanding Dues tab lists all customers who have pending balances, showing their phone numbers, last payment dates, and days elapsed since their last transaction. Shopkeepers can print an account statement or payment receipt in one click.

4. Thermal Receipt Printing

  • Standard 80mm & 58mm Support: Formatted specifically for ESC/POS thermal paper rolls.
  • Bilingual Header & Urdu Notes: Displays shop name, address, NTN, invoice number, cashier name, customer name (for credit bills), previous balance, and new total balance.
  • Custom Footer: Customizable message (e.g., "Khareeda hua maal 3 din mein wapis ho sakta hai").

Aaj Cash & Carry POS Section 5: Security & Master PIN

Security, Roles & Password Recovery

Commercial-grade role isolation preventing unauthorized cashier access, paired with an emergency recovery mechanism.

1. Strict Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)

Admin Account (Shop Owner)

  • Full access to Dashboard, Sales, & Profit charts
  • Cost prices, purchase invoices, & supplier dues
  • Digital Khata customer management & adjustments
  • Shop settings, cashier registration, & backups
  • Password management & Master Recovery PIN

Cashier Accounts (Billing Staff)

  • Locked strictly to the POS Billing checkout screen
  • Can scan items, accept cash/credit, and print receipts
  • CANNOT see product cost prices or shop profits
  • CANNOT edit settings or delete past sales
  • CANNOT access financial audit logs

2. Master Recovery PIN (ATM-Style Secret Code)

In typical retail shops, if the owner forgets their admin password, they often have to pay an expensive technician or re-install the software, losing their records. Aaj POS solves this with a built-in Master Recovery PIN:

How the Master PIN Works:

  1. The owner configures a secret 4 to 6 digit PIN (e.g., 7860) inside Shop Settings.
  2. If the admin password is ever forgotten, the owner clicks "Forgot Password?" on the login screen.
  3. The software prompts for the secret Master PIN. Once verified, the owner immediately types a new password and logs in.

3. Desktop Emergency Reset Tool (emergency-reset-admin.bat)

If the owner forgets both their password and their Master Recovery PIN, they can double-click the included emergency-reset-admin.bat batch file directly on their Windows desktop.

  • Resets admin credentials back to default (admin / 1234) without touching any sales, product, or customer khata data.
  • Requires physical access to the Windows operating system file system, preventing remote or cashier tampering.

4. Complete Audit Trail (audit_logs)

Every sensitive operation (password change, Master PIN update, database backup, database restore, manual khata adjustment, and sale deletion) is permanently recorded with timestamp and username.

Aaj Cash & Carry POS Section 7: Buyer Deployment Guide

Buyer Setup & Deployment Guide

How to install and set up this POS on a client's computer in under 10 minutes.

Step-by-Step Installation Process

Step 1: Run the Windows Installer

Copy Aaj Cash & Carry POS Setup 1.0.0.exe to the buyer's computer (via USB or download) and double-click to install. It automatically creates a desktop shortcut.

Step 2: Initial Admin Login

Open the app. Select Role: Admin. Log in using default credentials:
Username: admin  |  Password: 1234

Step 3: Configure Shop Branding & Master PIN

Go to Shop Settings:
• Update Shop Name, Phone Number, Address, and upload the store logo.
• Change the default admin password to the owner's personal password.
• Set a 4 to 6 digit Master Recovery PIN (e.g. 7860).

Step 4: Create Cashier Accounts

Go to Register Cashier. Create staff accounts (e.g., toseef, kashif) with secure passwords. Cashiers cannot see store profits or settings.

Step 5: Backup Routine (1-Click USB Backup)

Advise the shopkeeper to plug in a USB flash drive once a week, go to Shop Settings → Offline Database Backup, and click "Backup Database Now". A single .db file will save all their products, sales, and customer khata records safely.

Ready to Deploy & Commercialize: This application is 100% turnkey. No ongoing server maintenance, no external database administration, and zero technical overhead for the retailer.
